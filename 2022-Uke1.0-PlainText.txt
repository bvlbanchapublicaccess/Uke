NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises and covenants contained herein, and intending to be legally bound hereby, the Parties agree as follows:
1. Engagement and Scope of Services. Subject to the terms and conditions set forth in this Agreement, Knowledge Holder hereby engages Requestor to perform the services of videoing and/or otherwise recording Knowledge Holder’s stories, narratives, information, and the like for the Knowledge Holder to be used any way the Knowledge Holder sees fit (the “Services”). The Services may be modified or expanded upon written agreement of the Parties.
2. Compensation. The Parties agree the opportunity of allowing Interviewer to record and work with Knowledge Holder shall be full compensation for the Services and no additional fees shall be owed. It is Requestor’s hope and desire that Knowledge Holder may permit Interviewer to use the videos or recordings for Interviewer’s Purpose, to be set forth in a separate agreement.


3. Term. The term of this Agreement will commence on the Effective Date set forth above and will continue in full force and effect until the Services are completed to the satisfaction and approval of Knowledge Holder (the “Term”).
4. Intellectual Property Rights. All rights, title, and interest, including copyright interest, in any recording or other work done by Requestor that is discovered, developed, learned, created, produced, or provided by Requestor, alone or in combination with any Requestor or employee of Knowledge Holder, that pertain in any way to the Services, and whether arising prior to or during the Term, are the property of Knowledge Holder. Requestor agrees that any contributions by Interviewer to the creation of such works, including all copyright interest therein, shall be considered works made for hire by Requestor for Knowledge Holder under 17 U.S.C. § 101 and that such work shall, upon their creation, be owned exclusively by Knowledge Holder. To the extent that any such works may not be considered works made for hire for Knowledge Holder under applicable law, Requestor agrees to assign and, upon their creation, automatically assigns to Knowledge Holder, the ownership of such works, including copyright interests and any other intellectual property therein, without the necessity of any further consideration.

Neither party will be responsible for the acts or omissions of the other party hereto nor the acts or omissions of the employees of the other party hereto, except for acts or omissions of the other party or such other party’s employees caused by the grossly negligent or willful acts or omissions of such party. Neither party shall have the authority to speak for, represent or obligate the other party hereto in any way without either the express prior written consent of or written ratification of the other party.
6. Indemnification. Requestor agrees to indemnify, defend and hold Knowledge Holder harmless from and against any liability arising out of or in connection with (i) the negligent or willful acts of Requestor in the performance of Requestor’s obligations hereunder, (ii) allegations of infringement of intellectual property, or (iii) the breach by Requestor of any of Requestor’s obligations hereunder.
7. Entire Agreement. This Agreement represents the entire agreement and understanding of the Parties with respect to the subject matter of this Agreement.
